Chrome-Automation

Privacy policy

Effective September 26, 2026. Chrome-Automation is a personal publishing application operated by Paulo Santos.

Google Drive access

The application requests read-only Google Drive permission to find and download article-source JSON files from folders selected in the operator's configuration. Although that permission can read files throughout the authorized Drive account, the automated download job targets only its configured folders. It does not modify or delete files on Google Drive.

Use and storage

Downloaded article text and file metadata are stored on the operator-controlled computer and used to prepare articles and podcasts. Processing records and operational logs may retain file names, source information, and publication status. OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored in the operator's local rclone configuration to allow scheduled downloads.

Processing and publication

Article content may be passed to text-generation or publishing services configured by the operator as part of the article and podcast workflow. Content selected for publication may become public on the publishing platform. Google Drive OAuth tokens are used for access to Google APIs and are not included in published content. This is not a service for collecting other users' private Drive documents.

Retention and control

Downloaded files, generated content, logs, and processing records remain on the operator's systems until removed through the workflow or by the operator. Revoking Google access stops future authorized downloads but does not delete existing local copies or published material. The operator can remove the application's access in Google Account third-party connections and delete local credentials and retained files separately.
