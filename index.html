Chrome-Automation

A personal article and podcast publishing workflow operated by Paulo Santos.

The application reads article-source JSON files from configured Google Drive folders, downloads them to an operator-controlled computer, and prepares articles and audio for the operator's publishing workflow.

Google Drive access supports downloading source files and moving verified published source JSONs to Google Drive Trash. Cleanup matches publication records and source content before moving a file. Access is intended for the operator's own account.
